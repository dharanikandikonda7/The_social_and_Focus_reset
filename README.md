## 📖 Book Overview

**The Social & Focus Reset** is a research-backed, practical 7-day workbook designed to help readers escape the dopamine loop of modern smartphones, reclaim lost time, and build commanding real-life social presence. 

Rather than advocating for extreme, unsustainable "digital detoxes", this workbook provides practical **friction tweaks**, **environment redesigns**, and **daily actionable worksheets** grounded in cognitive psychology and neuroscience research from Harvard, UC Irvine, JAMA, and PNAS.

### 🗓️ Day-by-Day Breakdown

* **Day 1: Awareness Audit (Know Your Baseline)**
  * Measure your true screen time, pickup count, and identify your top 3 autopilot phone habits.
  * *Key Action:* The Screen Time Selfie & Autopilot Tracker.
* **Day 2: Environment Design (Make Focus Default)**
  * Turn on grayscale, remove social media apps from the home screen, and set up physical boundaries.
  * *Key Action:* The 3-Foot Distance Rule & Bedroom Charger Isolation.
* **Day 3: The Focus Muscle (Single-Tasking & Deep Work)**
  * Train sustained attention with 25-minute Pomodoro cycles and zero browser tab switching.
  * *Key Action:* The Single-Tab Challenge & Refocus Protocol.
* **Day 4: Boundaries & Triggers (Catch Urges Early)**
  * Uncover your top scroll triggers (boredom, waiting, stress) and build a 10-second replacement buffer.
  * *Key Action:* The "Name It To Tame It" Urge Sheet.
* **Day 5: Real-Life Reconnection (Stop Phubbing)**
  * Trade screen scrolling for real-world presence. Have a phone-free meal and make a voice call instead of texting.
  * *Key Action:* The 2-Minute Update Rule & Eye Contact Practice.
* **Day 6: New Rituals (Own the Bookends of Your Day)**
  * Zero phone use during your first 30 morning minutes and last 60 evening minutes to protect melatonin and mental clarity.
  * *Key Action:* The Morning & Night Ritual Card.
* **Day 7: Integration (Lock In Permanent Gains)**
  * Compare Day 1 vs Day 7 numbers, select your top 3 permanent rules, and write your personal Digital Constitution.
  * *Key Action:* Your Written Digital Constitution.
